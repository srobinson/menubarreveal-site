# Menu Bar Reveal

> A single icon for your menu bar.

## What it does

Menu Bar Reveal is a free Mac app that shows the hidden menu bar when you press your keyboard shortcut. The bar stays until the same shortcut hides it.

## Shortcut

You choose it: there is no default. The first launch asks for one, and you can change it from the app's menu bar icon.

## Compatibility

Requires macOS 14 or later, with the menu bar set to hide automatically.

## Install

Download [version 1.0.1 as a DMG](https://github.com/srobinson/menubar-reveal/releases/download/v1.0.1/MenuBarReveal-1.0.1.dmg) and drag Menu Bar Reveal to Applications. Apple has not notarised it, so macOS blocks the first open. The [README](https://github.com/srobinson/menubar-reveal#readme) gives the two ways past that. It starts at login unless you turn that off in its menu.

## Price and licence

Free, with no account, trial or paid tier. Open source under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/srobinson/menubar-reveal/blob/v1.0.1/LICENSE).

## Privacy

No network code and no analytics. It needs no Accessibility, Input Monitoring or Screen Recording permission. The [technical notes](https://github.com/srobinson/menubar-reveal/blob/v1.0.1/FACTS.md) say how it works.
